# PACT360 Administrator Guide

This guide is for Super Admins and System Administrators of **PACT360**.

---

## 👥 Managing Users & Access (RBAC)

1. Open **System Configuration** from the sidebar navigation.
2. Under the **User Accounts** tab, you can view the complete active roster.
3. Click **Create User** to register a new employee. Input their name, email, default password, office location, and choose their RBAC role designation.
4. You can edit any existing user's profile to change their department, suspend their account (changing status to `Suspended`), or reset their login credentials.

---

## 🏢 Operations Structure

### 1. Adding Offices & Physical Locations
- Navigate to the **Offices & Locations** tab.
- Enter a name (e.g. Lofa Field Office), type (Field Office or Warehouse), and code (e.g. LFO).
- Once saved, this location is immediately available as a filter when registering or transferring assets.

### 2. Department Management
- Navigate to the **Departments** tab to configure organizational structures (e.g. WASH Program, Human Resources, Logistics).

---

## ⚙️ Global Preferences

Open the **System Settings** tab to adjust overall system behavior:
- **Organization Name**: Customizes header branding across the app.
- **Asset / Contract Prefix**: Controls automatic numbering formats (e.g. change `PLAN-AST-` to `PLAN-LBR-AST-`).
- **Alert Thresholds**: Configures how many days in advance the app flags contract expirations, warranty expirations, and overdue maintenance schedules.
