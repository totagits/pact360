# Physical Inventory Audit Protocol

This document covers workflows for executing field inventory reconciliations.

## Phase 1: Planning
1. Create a Campaign in "Physical Audits" naming the target warehouse or office.
2. Select start/end dates and assign audit team members.

## Phase 2: Counting
1. Logistics teams visit locations with mobile or tablet devices.
2. Open the reconciliation checklist.
3. Check the serial number/tag against the database.
4. Log status ("Verified", "Missing", "Damaged") and notes.

## Phase 3: Reporting
1. Generate the discrepancy variance report.
2. Head of Operations reviews missing assets for investigation.
